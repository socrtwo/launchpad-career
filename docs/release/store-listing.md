## Name

Launchpad Career

## Short description (80 characters)

Turn one career goal into a realistic plan. No account needed.

## Full description

Launchpad Career helps students, recent graduates and career changers turn one goal into
a plan they can act on.

Start with what you want help with:

- Picking a direction
- Choosing a major or graduate path
- Finding an internship or job
- Improving your resume and LinkedIn profile
- Preparing for interviews
- Building skills employers are looking for

Then use the tools that fit:

- Browse occupations and read what the work involves and the ways in, including routes
  that do not need a four-year degree.
- Compare two or three occupations side by side.
- See a short list matched to your answers, with the reason for each one shown.
- Build a step-by-step plan towards an occupation.
- Plan a week of job searching around the time you have.
- Work through your resume and LinkedIn profile section by section.
- Practise common interview questions with space for your own examples.
- See what you have done so far.

What it does not do. It does not promise admission, a job or a salary. It is not a job
board. It is not a counselling or mental-health service, and it gives no medical,
psychological or legal advice.

Your data. You do not need an account. Nothing you enter is saved unless you choose to
save it on your device, and you can see or delete it at any time. There is no advertising
and no tracking.

About the content. Occupation descriptions and guidance were drafted with AI assistance
and are labelled as drafts until a careers adviser has reviewed them. Suggestions are made
by fixed rules from your answers, not by a prediction about you.
